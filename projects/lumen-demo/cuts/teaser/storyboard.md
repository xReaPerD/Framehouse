# Lumen Demo — `teaser` storyboard

**Canvas:** 1920×1080 · **Target length:** 15s · **FPS:** 30
**Format guide:** `references/formats/teaser.md` · **Brief:** `../../brief.md`
**Visual board:** `storyboard/board.html` (frames in `storyboard/frames/`)

## Spine
- **Hook (first 2s):** total darkness → one amber point of light appears and blooms.
- **Arc:** spark → listening (light reacts) → glimpse of the lamp (arc + cone, never the full product) → focus (cone tightens to a pool) → breath → the light becomes the wordmark's full stop → end card.
- **Hero object:** the point of light. It is never off screen; every beat is a shape it turns into.
- **Music / pacing:** synthesized warm pad, ~70 BPM feel, swells to 11.0 s, drops to near-silence for the breath (11.0–11.8), one soft whoosh into the wordmark at 11.8, pad tail fades out 13.5–15.0.
