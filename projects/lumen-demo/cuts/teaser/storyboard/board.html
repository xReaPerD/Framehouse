Warm near-black, never pure #000, so the amber glow has something to melt into. Amber stays under 10% of the frame.

Type (proposed)

Light that listens.
Outfit 500 / 300 · Coming soon

One geometric sans, two weights. Lines are sentence case, centred, tracked tight, and blur in word by word.

Motion & sound

Motion: slow and weightless; light moves like liquid. Blooms on expo.out, moves on power3.inOut. Nothing snaps.

Sound: a warm synth pad and one soft whoosh, both synthesized locally with FFmpeg, so they're original and royalty-free. The film also works silent.
