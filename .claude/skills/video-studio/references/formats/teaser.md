# Teaser (6–30 s, typically 15 s)

Purpose: create curiosity, **withhold** the full reveal. Shows less than a trailer, on purpose.

## Structure (15 s)
| Time | Beat |
| --- | --- |
| 0–2 s | Hook — an intriguing fragment (extreme close-up, silhouette, a sound, a question). |
| 2–9 s | 2–4 glimpses that raise questions, not answer them. Fast, partial, cropped, obscured. |
| 9–12 s | One line of text that frames the mystery. |
| 12–15 s | Title / logo + date or "Coming soon". Hold ≥ 2.5 s. |

6 s bumper: hook (2 s) → one glimpse (2 s) → title + date (2 s).

## Rules
- Reuse the trailer's visual language if one exists (same fonts, palette, title treatment).
- Prefer darkness, negative space, crops and sound design over exposition.
- If it's for social, also cut a `teaser-vertical` (portrait) — recompose, don't just crop.
