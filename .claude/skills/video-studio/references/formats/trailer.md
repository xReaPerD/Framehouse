# Trailer (45–120 s, typically 60 s, 16:9)

Purpose: make the viewer *want* it — a game, film, product, event — and tell them what to do next.

## Three-act structure (60 s)
| Act | Time | Job | Typical content |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cold open | 0–6 s | Hook | Single striking image or line; often quiet. |
| Act 1 — World | 6–20 s | Set up | What it is, the setting/problem. Medium pace, 2–3 s shots. Title card 1. |
| Act 2 — Escalation | 20–45 s | Build | Features / conflict / stakes. Cuts get faster, music builds, text cards punctuate. |
| Breath | ~45 s | Contrast | 0.5–1 s of silence or a held frame. |
| Act 3 — Climax | 45–54 s | Payoff | Fastest montage on the drop, the money shot, the logo/title reveal. |
| Button + end card | 54–60 s | Act | Short sting / joke / final beat, then logo + CTA + date held ≥ 2.5 s. |

Scale proportionally for 90 s / 120 s; add a second escalation rather than lengthening shots.

## Text cards
3–5 cards total, building a sentence across the trailer ("THIS SUMMER" … "ONE CHOICE" … "CHANGES
EVERYTHING"). Same typographic style each time; animate in on a beat.

## Checklist
- [ ] Hook works muted · [ ] title appears before 60 % mark and again at the end
- [ ] music drop aligned to climax · [ ] end card has CTA + date/URL · [ ] no invented claims
